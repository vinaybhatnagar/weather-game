Frequently asked questions

Is PixForge really free?
Yes—PixForge is 100% free to use with no sign‑up. If you find it helpful, consider sharing it or starring the project on GitHub.
Do my images get uploaded?
No. All editing happens locally in your browser. Your images and edits never leave your device.
Which file types are supported?
You can import PNG, JPG, WEBP, and SVG. Exports support PNG, JPG, and WEBP. Transparency is supported.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After your first visit, most features work offline thanks to browser caching. For best results, add it to your home screen.
